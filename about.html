About

Hi, I'm Moe Thet Hnin.

I help business teams and developers understand each other — and then I make sure the project gets done.

I spent over seven years in web direction and CMS operations for a major pharmaceutical company, running 21 brand sites on AEM and leading the migration of 7 existing sites plus a new one to a new CMS. Coordinating specs in English with global teams was part of everyday work.

I then moved into business-system QA and customer support, which sharpened my eye for quality and for what users really need. Today I lead a 7-member team on generative AI and automation projects, where one rollout alone cut about 16 hours of month-start work.
