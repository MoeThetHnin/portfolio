AI Business Improvement & Automation (FDE)

PoCs and system development that use generative AI and automation to streamline internal work — including overtime pay calculation, monthly performance-data aggregation and automatic journal entries from bank transactions.

Period
Oct 2026 – Present
Role
Project Leader
Team
8 (leader + 7)
  • Python
  • Azure OpenAI Service
  • Microsoft Copilot
  • Claude
~16hrs
saved every month-start by automating Excel data aggregation

Challenge

Month-start aggregation was done by hand and collided with other deadlines, causing heavy workloads and input errors. The developers had no business-domain knowledge, which put the project at risk of rework.

What I did

  • Run the whole project: planning, scheduling and issue management
  • Assign tasks, track progress and coach 7 team members
  • Interview business teams to surface issues, then select and prioritize AI themes
  • Document current workflows and rules as guidelines developers can build from
  • Define, validate and roll out automated data aggregation; drive the bank-journal PoC
  • Facilitate meetings, build consensus and report progress

Results

  • Cut month-start aggregation work by about 16 hours
  • Removed manual input — fewer errors and deadlines met
  • Guidelines kept development moving with minimal rework
  • Users said the work became “far less stressful” and “a big help”
